FAQ

Questions? Answered.

What's your design process like?
I follow a human-centered design approach: discover → define → ideate → prototype → test → iterate. I start with deep research, then move to wireframes, high-fidelity mockups, and interactive prototypes before handoff.
How long does a typical project take?
It depends on scope. A landing page might take 1–2 weeks, while a full app design could take 4–8 weeks. I always provide a clear timeline upfront and keep you updated throughout.
Do you also do development?
I primarily design, but I can build pixel-perfect front-end code using HTML, CSS, Tailwind, and JavaScript. For complex apps, I collaborate with trusted developers.
How do we get started?
Just reach out via the contact form or email. We'll schedule a quick call to discuss your goals, timeline, and budget. Then I'll send a proposal and we can kick off!
