The Problem

A mess runs on paper. Trust runs on proof.

Shanti Niketan Hostel houses 120 students. Every meal, expense, and deposit lives in three paper registers and a WhatsApp group. When students argue about charges, no one can prove anything.

I just don't go. They never know, but I still get charged. Aditya Verma (P2) · Student, 21 · Shanti Niketan Hostel

Three symptoms appeared consistently across every user role: students charged for meals they skipped, managers blamed for expenses they never recorded, and wardens unable to verify cleaning they never saw.

All three trace to a single root cause — no shared, verifiable source of truth.

Key Findings

Three root causes. Every visible symptom.

Each finding is traced to a quote, an observation, and a quantified impact. Every HMW became a design directive.

01

The Silent Skip

S1 · Critical
"I just don't go. They never know, but I still get charged." — Aditya Verma (P2), Student, 21 · Shanti Niketan Hostel

Evidence

  • Full-day shadow: 167 registered meals, only 133 served — 34 wasted
  • Diary study (n=63): 21 skips, only 5 communicated (24%)
  • 4 of 5 students couldn't state the cutoff rule

Impact

  • ₹2,040 lost in a single day at ₹60/meal
  • Projected 15–25% monthly food waste
  • 15–20 disputes per month (manager estimate)
HMW Make the skip signal effortless — and unignorable — before cutoff.
02

The Expense Blind Spot

S1 · Critical
"I record it later. Sometimes I forget. Then the month doesn't add up and everyone blames me." — Suresh Kumar (P7), Mess Manager, 45 · 12 years experience

Evidence

  • 15-day register audit: ₹3,200 in unrecorded expenses
  • Manager maintains 3 separate registers
  • Warden sees only monthly total — never line items

Impact

  • ~₹6,400/month projected financial leakage
  • Warden cannot audit or verify spending
  • Manager blamed for a system failure
HMW Make recording an expense faster than forgetting it.
03

The Proof Deficit

S2 · High
"How do I know the bathroom was cleaned? I don't. I just have to trust them." — Mrs. Sunita Sharma (P9), Hostel Warden, 50 · 3 messes, 200 students

Evidence

  • Cleaning happens daily — never recorded. No checklist, no timestamp, no photo
  • Deposits tracked on paper; students receive no receipt
  • Disputes become "he said, she said"

Impact

  • Warden cannot enforce hygiene standards
  • Deposit disputes are unresolvable
  • Students feel powerless — even when they're right
HMW Make invisible work visible — with minimal effort from the person doing it.

Design Solutions

Every decision traces back to evidence.

Not features looking for problems — problems demanding specific solutions. Each one defended by evidence from testing.

Solves Finding 01

One-Tap Meal Toggle

No form, no dropdown, no confirmation dialog. One tap on the meal card with a 2-second undo toast. Cutoff shown prominently. Push reminder at 7 PM for 9 PM cutoff, SMS fallback.

Evidence3/3 students completed unassisted in 4.2s average.
Solves Finding 06

Itemized Balance View

Never show a single number. Always show the breakdown — deposited, meals × rate, extra charges, remaining. Each line tappable. Download statement for dispute proof.

Evidence3/3 could explain the math, vs. 17% survey baseline.
Solves Finding 02

10-Second Expense Entry

Category chips, numeric keypad, optional photo. Save. Done. If amount exceeds ₹5,000, auto-escalates to warden. Assumption flagged for future validation.

EvidenceManager completed entry in 8.7s (n=1; needs wider validation).
Solves Finding 03

Photo-Proof Cleaning Log

Daily checklist — Bathroom 1, Bathroom 2, Kitchen, Corridor. Tap task, camera opens, take photo, submit. Timestamp auto-recorded. Missed tasks flagged red for warden.

EvidenceWarden identified all 3 missed days in 6 seconds.
New · From Findings

Dispute Resolution Flow

Student taps charge → "I disagree" → selects reason → adds note. Manager accepts or rejects. If unresolved in 48h, escalates to warden. Both notified at every step.

StatusDesigned but not yet tested — flagged for next round.
Solves Finding 05

Cook Mode

Giant number. "LUNCH — 60 plates." Hindi + regional language. Optional voice announcement. No navigation, no text beyond the number. Designed for a user who can't read English.

StatusDesigned from one chat — needs testing with 3 cooks.

10 / 11
Reflection

What I'd do differently. What surprised me.

Honest critique is what separates junior portfolios from hire-worthy ones. These are the limits of this project — stated plainly.

✦

What went well

  • Contextual inquiry was worth 10 interviews — surfaced ₹3,200 in unrecorded expenses no interview would have found.
  • The "trust" lens unified every finding and design decision.
  • The cook interview changed the design — the lowest-tech user needed the simplest screen.
⟳

What I'd redo

  • I assumed cutoff rules were clear. 4/5 students couldn't state them.
  • Tested only 5 users across 3 roles — needed 3 per role (12 total).
  • Cook screen is unvalidated — designed from one informal chat.
✧

What surprised me

  • Students don't want more features. They want fewer numbers they can't verify.
  • Managers don't resist technology. They resist technology slower than their register.
  • Wardens don't want dashboards. They want proof they can check in 2 minutes.
